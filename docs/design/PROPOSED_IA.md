# Proposed IA

StudyHub should answer four student questions:

- Home: What should I continue now?
- Courses: Where are my materials?
- Search: Where is something I remember?
- Study: What should I practice or review?
- AI: What was I discussing, and can AI help me understand this?
- Settings: How is my local library configured?

Primary navigation:

- Home
- Courses
- Search
- Study
- AI
- Settings

Secondary workflows:

- Starred files become a filter/section under Courses.
- Practice, Wrong Questions, and Exam Review live under Study.
- Scan and Add Material live in Courses and Settings.
- File/course/week/question Ask actions all open the same persistent AI conversation system.

Routing compatibility:

- Existing internal route names remain available where cheap.
- `askgpt` maps to the AI page.
- `practice`, `wrong`, and `exam` map into Study modes.
