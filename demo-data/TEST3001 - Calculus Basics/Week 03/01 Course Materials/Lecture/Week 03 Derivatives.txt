Topic: Derivatives

The derivative of a function at a point measures the instantaneous rate of change. For a smooth curve, it is the slope of the tangent line at that point.

Power rule:
If f(x) = x^n, then f'(x) = n x^(n-1) for constant n.

Rendered math example:
$f'(x)=n x^{n-1}$ and $$\frac{d}{dx}x^4 = 4x^3$$.
