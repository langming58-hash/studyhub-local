Week 01 Tutorial Solutions

Solution Q1:
Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative forgone. For the student, it may include the wage from paid work, experience from the shift, or rest time given up to revise.

Solution Q2:
Demand may increase because the lower price makes coffee more attractive relative to substitutes. Total revenue may not increase if the price fall is proportionally larger than the quantity increase.

Solution Q3:
Excess demand means buyers want more than sellers are willing to supply at the current price. Competition among buyers can push the price upward until quantity demanded and supplied are closer.
